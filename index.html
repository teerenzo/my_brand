About Me

Senior Software Engineer & Technical Leader

As a Senior Software Engineer with 6+ years of experience, I specialize in designing, developing, and deploying innovative web and mobile applications. My expertise spans full-stack development, mobile development with Flutter, and team leadership across various technology stacks.

I have successfully led cross-functional teams, mentored over 300 students in software development, and delivered high-quality applications for companies like Codeit Rwanda, Ikanisa Ltd, and HuzaLabs. My technical leadership has resulted in improved project delivery timelines and enhanced team productivity.

Passionate about emerging technologies, I stay current with industry trends and have experience with modern frameworks including ReactJS, NextJS, NodeJS, Flutter, and various databases. I hold certifications in Google Cloud, SpringBoot, and Andela Technical Leadership Program.
